Why I Paint

Computer science is all about logic and getting things exactly right. Painting is the opposite: there's no compiler yelling at me, and a “mistake” can turn into the best part of the piece. It's how I switch my brain off after a long day of code.

Advice I'd Give a Beginner

The best tip anyone ever gave me:

Paint what you love, not what you think you're supposed to paint.

My three rules for a painting session
  1. Put on a good playlist.
  2. Keep the water cup far away from the coffee cup.
  3. Don't judge a painting until it's dry.
