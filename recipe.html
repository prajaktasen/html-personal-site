Thai Red Chicken Curry

Creamy, a little spicy, and ready in about half an hour. This is my go-to dinner when I want something cozy that tastes like way more effort than it actually is.

At a Glance

  • Prep time: 10 minutes
  • Cook time: 20 minutes
  • Spice level: medium (add more curry paste if you're brave)

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp Thai red curry paste
  • 500 g (about 1 lb) boneless chicken, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 can (400 ml) coconut milk
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • Juice of ½ a lime
  • A handful of fresh basil leaves
  • Cooked rice, to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the curry paste and stir for about 1 minute, until it smells amazing.
  3. Add the chicken and cook for 5 minutes, stirring so it gets coated in the paste.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk, add the bell pepper, and bring it to a gentle simmer.
  5. Simmer for 10–12 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce thickens.
  6. Stir in the fish sauce, brown sugar, and lime juice. Taste and adjust.
  7. Turn off the heat, stir in the basil, and serve over rice.

Tips & Fun Facts

Frying the curry paste in oil first is the secret to a deeper flavour.
The heat in chillies comes from capsaicin, C18H27NO3 — and it dissolves in fat, which is why the coconut milk makes this curry feel less spicy.

The 1st time I made this, I used way too much curry paste. Now I start with less and add more at the end.
